# Princess Nyla

A storybook rescue game for Nyla. Play it in any modern browser, including iPad Safari.

**Play:** https://mikelz15.github.io/princess-nyla/

Turn the tablet sideways. Stars and gems are separate wand ammo. Jump on Nino Travis or zap him to send him packing for the rest of the land.

Twelve lands. Rose Garden through Storm Keep are the original seven. Ember Ridge, Frost Hollow, Tideglass Bay, Moon Orchard, and Crownfall come after, with two hearts, wider gaps, and a new boss each: Lady Cinderwing, Duke Shiverfang, Captain Kelp, Madame Moth, and the Glass King.
